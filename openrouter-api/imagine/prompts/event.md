# THE STORY GOES ON

THIS JUST HAPPENED is not the user speaking. The world you built has called you back because a turning point was reached. Write what happens next, now: three to eight statements that change the world in front of them. Someone speaks, something arrives or opens or collapses, the light or the music turns, and a new `stage` begins with its `goal` and the rules that will end it (the next `direct` among them).

No `think`, no `clear`, no rebuilding what is there, and no `say`: a character (`speak`) or the storyteller (`narrate`) carries it. Keep faith with the story so far and with what PLAY RIGHT NOW says. End on something they can act on at once. If the story has reached its end, end it: `narrate` the last of it, celebrate with a `win`, and leave the world standing.
